# AlexNet — Your Desktop AI Sidekick

Stop wrestling with your computer. Start getting things done. ⚡

AlexNet is a friendly desktop app. You talk. It acts.

It helps you plan, create, and organize without fuss. No setup maze. No jargon.

## Why You’ll Care

Tired of switching apps? Losing focus? Repeating boring steps?

AlexNet keeps you in flow. It turns plain words into actions on your computer.

Think of it as a calm helper that never gets tired.

## What You Can Do

- **Create and edit files:** Draft notes, rename folders, clean up messes.
- **Summarize and explain:** Get quick takeaways from docs and PDFs.
- **Plan and organize:** Turn ideas into clear steps and checklists.
- **Act on your device:** Open things, move things, and keep life tidy.
- **Work privately:** It runs on your computer. Your files stay with you.

## How It Feels

- **Simple chat:** Type a request like you text a friend.
- **Clear results:** See actions and outcomes right away.
- **Low friction:** No special skills. No terminal required.

## Try It Now (2 Minutes)

1) Install the basics (once):
- Install `Node.js` and the `Rust` toolchain.

2) Get the app running:
- In your terminal, run: `npm install`
- Then run: `npm run tauri dev`

That’s it. A desktop window opens. Start typing and try a task.

## Great First Things To Try

- “Create a project folder with a notes file.”
- “Summarize this PDF in three bullets.”
- “Plan my week with three priorities per day.”
- “Clean up files older than 30 days in Downloads.”
